# Addition of Two Integers

Adding two integers means combining their values to find a total sum.

Example: 5 + 3 = 8, In this example, 5 and 3 are the two integers, and 8 is their sum.
Another example: 6 + seven = 13, In this example, 6 and 7 are the two integers, and 13 is their sum.

Example

seven x 3 = 21
This means add seven three times
seven + seven + seven = 21
